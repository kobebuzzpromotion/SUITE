## Conditions preserved

- [Crop](https://support.evoto.ai/crop/) ordinary Expand fills gray, white or black, with 200% per-side and 13000 × 13000 px limits. Default-on Auto Expand During Sync is separate from [AI Lab](https://support.evoto.ai/ai-lab/) Generative Expand and its three candidates. Orientation-dependent crop sync only supports inverse ratio pairs. AI Headshot Crop rejects multiple people / faces and face angles above 75°.
- [Eyes](https://support.evoto.ai/eyes/) confirms distinct appearance, eye symmetry and iris/gaze controls. Unsupported tinted glasses and non-quantified side-profile limits remain explicit.
- [Masking](https://support.evoto.ai/feature-introduction-masking/) supports individual editing, but Person preset/sync is global rather than per individual. Hidden masks exclude effects from export. [Black & White Edge Removal](https://support.evoto.ai/backdrop-changer/) sync excludes manual brush/eraser corrections.
- [AI Lab](https://support.evoto.ai/ai-lab/) Batch Edit lists People Removal, Old Photo Restoration and Detail Enhance. Background Fusion has its own batch workflow. Arm Body Hair Removal is Asia-only. Belly Slimming, restoration and excessive detail enhancement have documented quality risks.
- [Pet leash removal](https://support.evoto.ai/pet-body/) is single-image only, produces a .tsq copy and consumes one credit when exporting that new version. Collars / harnesses require manual healing. [Teeth](https://support.evoto.ai/teeth/) flaws/edge correction excludes AI-generated smile teeth, and alignment is optimized for adult teeth.
- [Smile Generation](https://support.evoto.ai/facial-expression/) states five levels and also three levels/default 3 for closed-mouth/neutral results. These are retained as unresolved variants. Eyebrow/eyelash independent sides do not establish independent Contacts or every makeup component.
- Skin tone changes may recolor facial features; radiance protects those colors. Eyedropper, tan-line automatic standard/enhanced mode, frequency separation and Dodge & Burn methods are tracked separately.
